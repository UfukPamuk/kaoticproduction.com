About the studio

Organised chaos, colourful results.

Kaotic Production was founded in Istanbul by Ufuk Pamuk. We're a tiny team, so every detail gets our attention: the colours, the sounds and the little moments that make you smile.

We want our games to be easy to pick up, kind to play and fair to the people who play them. Reverdo, a game about cleaning up the world and watching it turn green again, is the first one.

  • Based inIstanbul, Türkiye
  • FounderUfuk Pamuk
  • First gameReverdo
  • PlatformsPC (Steam), iOS, Android

For everyone

No violence, no chat, nothing to worry about: games the whole family can enjoy together.

Fair play

No paid lucky draws. Rewards are fixed, and the whole game can be played without paying.

A little meaning

Gentle stories with something to say, like caring for nature and the places we share.
