Description

Reverdo is a calm, colourful cleanup game where you collect and recycle litter to make the world green again.

A grey beach buried in litter… Grab your vacuum and start cleaning! Every corner you clear comes back to life: grass grows, flowers bloom, birds return and people come back to the park. When a map is 100% clean, a short before/after film shows how far it has come. From a beach to Istanbul's Sultanahmet Square, the Eiffel Tower, the Pyramids of Giza and the Taj Mahal, players clean 20 maps with their own music, people and little stories.

History

Reverdo started in 2026 as a small 2D prototype about cleaning up a beach, called Kıyı (Turkish for “shore”). As it grew into a 3D game that travels from a beach to world landmarks, it was renamed Reverdo: “re” (again) + “verde” (green), a place turning green again. It is the first game by Kaotic Production, an independent studio from Istanbul founded by Ufuk Pamuk.

Features

  • Clean up litter and watch grey places turn green, with a before/after film at the end of every map
  • 20 maps, including Sultanahmet Square, the Eiffel Tower, the Pyramids of Giza and the Taj Mahal
  • Play as Ada or Ege, with hats, outfits, colourful vacuums and trails
  • Machines that level up: octopus-grab excavator, Clean-Bot, push scrubber, sponge mop, sea-cleaning boat, cleanup submarine and metal detector
  • Living maps: tour boats, ice-cream vans, crises, rain, the lodos wind and desert sandstorms
  • Grandpa Tosbi the sea turtle, animals to rescue, historic artifacts to collect and upcycling
  • For all ages: no violence, no chat, no account; plays offline
  • Accessibility: colour-blind-friendly markers, reduced motion, slow reading
  • 11 languages
