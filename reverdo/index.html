Clean & green

Every corner comes back to life

A grey beach buried in litter… Grab your vacuum and start cleaning! Every corner you clear comes back to life: grass grows, flowers bloom, birds return and people come back to the park.

When a map is 100% clean, a short before/after film shows how far it has come. Save and share the before/after picture.

Your hero

Play as Ada or Ege

Dress Ada or Ege with hats, outfits, colourful vacuums and trails. Holidays and seasonal events decorate the maps.

Find historic artifacts hidden across the maps, complete the sets, unlock costumes, and turn what you collect into benches, birdhouses and solar lamps.

Machines

Machines for the big jobs

Upgrade your vacuum and grow your bag. For heavy work, take the controls. Machines gain new parts as they level up, and you can see it.

  • Octopus-grab excavator
  • Clean-Bot
  • Push scrubber
  • Sponge mop
  • Sea-cleaning boat
  • Cleanup submarine
  • Metal detector

Life returns

Grandpa Tosbi and animal friends

Tosbi, a wise old sea turtle, travels with you. Free a hedgehog caught in plastic rings or a turtle tangled in a net.

As the maps get cleaner they fill with life: swans return to the pond, an ice-cream van visits the park, a tour boat sails past the beach and an excursion train pulls into the station.

Living maps

Storms, crises and big moments

  • Crises: a tipped oil cart, spilled rubble, gulls raiding the bins. Beat the clock!
  • Rain, the lodos wind and desert sandstorms: the wind blows fresh litter onto ground you've cleaned.
  • Signature moments: a fountain show in the park, fireworks at the funfair, fireflies at the camp, baby turtles on the beach.
  • Something every day: a daily cleanup, a login streak, weekly events and seasonal decorations.

FAQ

Questions & answers

When does Reverdo come out?

Reverdo launches in Türkiye on iPhone and Android in early December 2026, then worldwide on Steam, the App Store and Google Play in January 2027.

Which platforms is it on?

Windows PC on Steam, iPhone and iPad on the App Store, and Android phones and tablets on Google Play. On PC it is Windows only for now.

How much does it cost?

Steam: US$6.99, buy once. No ads and no in-app purchases; everything is earned by playing.

Phones: free to play. It offers optional in-app purchases (gem packs, machine packs, the Green Pass, and Reverdo Pro to play without ads) and contains ads: optional rewarded ads and one ad when a map is finished. The whole game can be played without buying anything.

Which languages does it support?

11 languages: English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Chinese, Hindi and Arabic. You can pick one in Settings.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Reverdo plays offline and needs no account. On phones, the optional ads and purchases need a connection.

Is it suitable for children?

Reverdo is made for all ages: no violence, no chat and no account. On phones the game asks for a birth year once, on first launch. Players under 13 get a parent question before every real-money purchase and only see non-personalised, age-appropriate ads. Details are in the privacy policy.

Can I use a controller?

On PC you can play with keyboard or a controller, with rebindable keys. On phones and tablets there are touch controls.
